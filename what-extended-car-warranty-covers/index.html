What an extended car warranty covers, and the 5 reasons claims get refused

Every product, every limit in dollars, and the five things that decide whether a claim is paid.

See my price, no phone number needed

An extended car warranty pays for eligible repairs when a covered mechanical or electrical part breaks down after the manufacturer's warranty ends. It isn't car insurance, so it doesn't cover accidents, theft or damage. Most refused claims come down to five things you can control.

Warranty or car insurance?

Extended warrantyCar insurance
Pays forEligible repairs when a covered part breaks downDamage from accidents, theft, fire, flood and similar events
ExampleGearbox fails at 150,000 kmCar damaged in a collision
ExcessNo upfront excess with Integrity's warranties; you pay above the approved amount or claim limitUsually an excess per claim

The 5 reasons claims get refused, and how to avoid each

ReasonWhat it meansHow to avoid it
1. The fault was already thereFaults that existed before you bought the car aren't coveredPrivate purchase: get the mechanic's condition report. Dealer purchase: ask for the inspection record
2. Wear and tearParts at the end of their normal life, such as brake pads, clutch linings, tyres and timing beltsBudget for these as running costs; wear and tear is excluded from every product
3. Repair started firstRepairs started without Integrity's authorisation aren't paidStop driving and call Integrity Claims on 03 9120 6666 before any work starts
4. Servicing overdueMore than 30 days or 1,000 km overdue makes the warranty inactiveSet a reminder; any licensed workshop can service it; keep every invoice
5. The repair is bigger than the limitEach product and level has a per-claim limitChoose the level whose limit matches the repairs you're worried about

Example: Integrity approves a $5,900 gearbox repair on Absolute Level 2. Integrity pays $5,000 and you pay $900. On Level 3 it pays the full $5,900.

What's covered depends on the product

  • Absolute and Total Cover: parts originally covered by the manufacturer's warranty, except the exclusions in the PDS.
  • Endurance: 20 named components, such as the engine, gearbox, differential, turbo, air conditioning, brakes and cooling system.
  • EV Warranty: listed components, including the battery and electric motor.

Commonly excluded parts (Absolute): brake pads and linings, disc rotors, tyres and wheels, batteries, filters, spark plugs, timing belts, exhaust systems, glass, trims, upholstery, software and other parts listed in the PDS.

How a claim works

Stop driving, call Integrity before any repair, Integrity directs you to an approved repairer, then pays the repairer directly for the approved amount. Diagnosis costs are yours unless they form part of an approved claim.

Cancelling

Full refund within 30 days of purchase or before cover starts, if no claim is paid. After that, a pro rata refund less claims paid and a $62.50 administration fee.
