Car warranty for high-kilometre and older cars

Cars of any age and any kilometres can have Endurance Option 1. Here is the price, what it pays per claim, and the one rule that keeps it active.

See my price, no phone number needed

Most extended warranties stop at a set age or kilometre reading. Endurance Option 1, issued by Integrity Car Care, has no age or kilometre limit. A 2008 hatchback on 310,000 km can have it. It costs $746 for 12 months or $1,121 for 5 years (about 61c a day), the same for every make (4WD and AWD add $110). You see your exact options and price online before you give us your number.

What it pays, in dollars

Endurance Option 1 pays up to $2,000 per claim for the engine, gearbox, differential and turbo, and up to $750 for the other 16 listed parts. There's no upfront excess.

Example: your gearbox fails and Integrity approves a $3,400 repair. Integrity pays the repairer $2,000 and you pay $1,400. Without cover, you pay $3,400.

Need a bigger limit? Option 2 raises it to $3,000 and $1,250 for cars up to 15 years old and under 200,000 km. See every Endurance price.

The one rule that keeps your cover active

Service every 6 months or 10,000 km on Options 1 and 2. If a service is more than 30 days or 1,000 km overdue, the warranty becomes inactive. Any licensed workshop can do it; keep every invoice.

Before you buy, have ready

  • A clear photo of the odometer
  • Your service records
  • Bought privately: a condition report from a licensed mechanic (how it works)

What isn't covered

Faults that existed before you bought the car, wear and tear, repairs started without Integrity's authorisation, missed servicing, and accident, flood, theft or misuse. A warranty is not car insurance. The full list is in the Endurance terms. Why claims get refused.
