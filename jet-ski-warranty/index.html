Extended warranty for jet skis, with every limit and price shown

Four levels and three terms. Here is who qualifies, what each level pays and what it costs.

Request a jet ski quote

The Integrity Jetski Warranty helps with the cost of repairing or replacing listed parts of your jet ski after a breakdown or failure. You choose a level from 1 to 4 and a term of 12, 36 or 60 months. The jet ski must be seaworthy when you buy the warranty. The warranty is issued by Integrity Car Care. If the jet ski is still under the manufacturer's warranty, cover begins when that ends.

This is not insurance. It is a discretionary risk product. Integrity decides whether to pay each claim and considers every claim on its merits. The claim limit is the most that can be paid for a listed part per claim, and an approved amount may be lower.

Extra benefits when a claim is approved

  • Towing: up to $100 per claim, if the jet ski's trailer can't be safely towed
  • Consequential damage to another listed part: up to $350 per claim

You pay first and are reimbursed on a paid invoice, under the conditions in the PDS.

Servicing you must keep up

Levels 1 to 3, jet ski up to 10 years old: a 4 stroke must be serviced every 12 months or 50 hours, a 2 stroke every 6 months or 25 hours, whichever comes first. Over 10 years old: a 4 stroke every 6 months or 25 hours, a 2 stroke every 6 months or 15 hours. Level 4: follow the manufacturer's schedule. Services must be done by a qualified jet ski service agent. If a service is more than 30 days or 5 hours late, the warranty becomes inactive. The cost of servicing is yours.

What isn't covered

The PDS excludes faults that were evident before you bought the jet ski or before the manufacturer's warranty ended, wear and tear, missed or late servicing, modifications outside the manufacturer's specification, accident, fire, theft, misuse, rust and corrosion, and repairs started without Integrity's approval. Water ingress, submersion and corrosion are excluded. So is use in racing, competitions or sporting events, and damage from poor quality or incorrect fuel. Only the listed part that failed first is covered, apart from the consequential damage benefit above. The full lists are in the Jetski Warranty PDS.

Claims, transfers and cancelling

  • Contact Integrity on 03 9120 6666 before any repair starts. Repairs started without approval are rejected.
  • You pay for diagnosis unless it is accepted as part of an approved claim.
  • If you sell, the warranty can be transferred to the new owner within 7 days for a $62.50 fee, with a condition report.
  • Full refund if you cancel within 30 days of purchase and no claim has been paid.
