Extended warranty for caravans, with every limit and price shown

Two cover types, three levels and five terms. Here is who qualifies, what each level pays and what it costs.

Request a caravan quote

The Integrity Caravan Warranty helps with the cost of repairing or replacing listed parts of your caravan after a breakdown or failure. You choose Caravan or Caravan Plus, a level from 1 to 3, and a term of 12, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months. The warranty is issued by Integrity Car Care. If the caravan is still under the manufacturer's warranty, cover begins when that ends.

This is not insurance. It is a discretionary risk product. Integrity decides whether to pay each claim and considers every claim on its merits. The claim limit is the most that can be paid for a listed part per claim, and an approved amount may be lower.

Extra benefits when a claim is approved

  • Accommodation: up to $150 a day and $500 per claim, if the caravan is immobilised for more than 1 day and more than 100 km from home
  • Towing: up to $100 per claim, if the caravan can't be safely towed by your vehicle
  • Consequential damage to another listed part: up to $350 per claim

You pay first and are reimbursed on a paid invoice, under the conditions in the PDS.

Servicing you must keep up

Your caravan must be serviced every 12 months by a qualified caravan service agent. Keep every service invoice. If a service is more than 30 days late, the warranty becomes inactive. The cost of servicing is yours.

What isn't covered

The PDS excludes faults that were evident before you bought the caravan or before the manufacturer's warranty ended, wear and tear, missed or late servicing, modifications outside the manufacturer's specification, accident, fire, theft, misuse, rust and corrosion, and repairs started without Integrity's approval. Damage from off-road use is excluded. So are batteries, tyres, glass, cosmetic items and fading or deterioration of paint, trim, curtains and awnings. Only the listed part that failed first is covered, apart from the consequential damage benefit above. The full lists are in the Caravan Warranty PDS.

Claims, transfers and cancelling

  • Contact Integrity on 03 9120 6666 before any repair starts. Repairs started without approval are rejected.
  • You pay for diagnosis unless it is accepted as part of an approved claim.
  • If you sell, the warranty can be transferred to the new owner within 7 days for a $62.50 fee, with a condition report.
  • Full refund if you cancel within 30 days of purchase and no claim has been paid.
