Our products

Choose the protection that fits your car

Extended car warranty

From $746 incl. GST for 1 year

Four warranties, all issued by Integrity Car Care. Your car's make, age, kilometres and fuel type decide which ones and which levels you can choose. The online quote shows every option your car qualifies for.

Absolute Warranty

Covers parts originally covered by the manufacturer's warranty, except the excluded parts and events in the terms. Four levels, per-claim limits from $2,000 (Level 1) to the market value of the vehicle (Level 4). 1 to 5 years.

Endurance Warranty

Covers 20 named components, such as the engine, gearbox and turbo. Option 1 has no age or kilometre limit. Per-claim limits from $750 to $7,000 depending on the part and option. 1, 3 or 5 years.

Total Cover

Comprehensive cover that also includes the hybrid system and electric motor. Plus, for hybrids and EVs, adds the electric motor battery. Three levels, $5,000, $8,000 or market value per claim. From $1,032, 1 to 5 years.

EV Warranty

For hybrid and fully electric vehicles. Lists the battery, electric motor and other components. Plan A has no age or kilometre limit. From $594, 1, 3 or 5 years.
  • 4WD and AWD vehicles: $110 surcharge
  • Servicing by any licensed mechanic, on the schedule in your terms
  • Bought privately? A vehicle condition report from a licensed mechanic is required (for Total Cover, unless you buy the car from a licensed dealer)

How buying works

From price to certificate, step by step

  1. Check your price

    Tell us the make, model, year and kilometres. You see the options your car qualifies for and the full price, including GST.

  2. Choose and pay

    Pick your cover and term. Roadside is optional and only added if you choose it. Pay securely by card.

  3. Send what's needed

    A clear photo of the odometer. If you bought privately, a vehicle condition report from a licensed mechanic. Upload at checkout or later from the link we email you.

  4. We lodge, Integrity confirms

    We check your details and lodge your warranty with Integrity Car Care, who issue your schedule and certificate. We email them to you, usually within 1 to 2 business days.

Paying does not start your cover on its own. Your warranty is in place once Integrity issues your schedule, from the start date shown on it. If your vehicle can't be accepted, we'll tell you and arrange a refund.

Roadside assistance key terms
  • Provided by Extended Dealer Services Pty Ltd (EDS), Australia-wide, 24 hours a day. Your membership confirmation gives the number to call.
  • 3 Star: 4 call-outs a year; towing up to 20 km (metro) or 50 km (country). 5 Star: unlimited call-outs (subject to excessive use); towing up to 50 km (metro) or 100 km (country), plus lockout help (you pay costs over $70), car hire up to $140 a day for 5 days, and accommodation benefits, when the conditions are met.
  • Both plans: jumpstarts and battery testing, flat tyre changes (a roadworthy spare is needed), and up to 10 litres of emergency fuel. Batteries, fuel and parts are at your cost.
  • Cover starts two working days after payment. If you need help before then, a $160 emergency join-up fee applies.
  • Membership fees are non-refundable. Towing covers vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes and 5.5 metres. Breakdowns caused by conditions that existed before your membership started are not covered.
