Who Integrity Car Care is

Integrity Car Care Pty Ltd (ABN 58 056 621 893) is a Victorian company based in Traralgon that issues mechanical breakdown warranties for used vehicles across Australia under Australian Financial Services Licence 247069. Its warranties are sold through car dealers and through authorised representatives. MyDriveShield (Authorised Representative No. 001323971) is one of those representatives: we arrange the cover, Integrity issues it and pays the claims. Dealer price vs the retail pricelist

The same Integrity warranty can be sold to you by a car dealer at the point of sale or by an authorised representative afterwards. The product, the PDS, the claim limits and the claims line are identical either way, because Integrity issues the warranty in both cases. What differs is the price and the process: a dealer sets its own price for the warranty and usually rolls it into the finance contract; the prices on this site are the retail pricelist figures, shown before you give any contact details, paid by card, and not financed. If a dealer has quoted you an Integrity warranty, the tables above tell you what the same cover costs here. Two warranties can't run together, so if you buy here the cover starts the day after any existing warranty ends.

How an Integrity claim works

  1. Stop driving if continuing could cause more damage.
  2. Call Integrity Claims on 03 9120 6666 (Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm AEST) or email claims@integritywarranty.com.au before any repair work starts.
  3. Integrity directs you to an approved repairer, or approves your own licensed workshop, who diagnoses the fault and sends the details through.
  4. Integrity assesses the claim and pays the repairer directly for the approved amount. There is no upfront excess; you pay anything above the approved amount or the claim limit. Diagnosis costs are yours unless they form part of an approved claim.

Claims are assessed against the PDS: pre-existing faults, wear and tear, missed servicing, modifications and accident or misuse damage are excluded. What a warranty covers, and the five reasons claims get refused.

Cancelling and transferring

Cancel by writing to Integrity at requests@integritywarranty.com.au. Within 30 days of purchase, or before cover starts, with no claims paid: full refund. After that: a pro rata refund for the unused days, less any accepted claims and a $62.50 administration fee. If you sell the car privately, the warranty can transfer to the buyer within 7 days of sale with Integrity's transfer form, a vehicle condition report and a $62.50 fee. It cannot be moved to another vehicle.
