Rules for pay links

  • Send a link only when the customer called us, asked for a callback, or is an existing customer or referral who asked us to call.
  • Factual information only. Do not recommend a product, level or term. If the customer asks what they should choose, explain the options and let them decide.
  • The customer must read the PDS and accept it at checkout. Do not tick it or pay for them.
  • Never take card details over the phone.
  • Complaints, claims, disputes, discounts and anything unusual go to the director.
